## ORTHO NYC ANNOATATIONS (LINK ONE)
*Note: These tiles are exported from ORTHO Results (2024)*

#### TO EXPORT:
	- Click left side arrow
	- Click current view to update the tiles which you are viewing (~ 0-150ft-300ft view zoomed in is optimal currently)
	- Click **ORTHO Results**
	- Click the dopdown to reveal the tile list
	- Click on the magnifying glass to see which tiles the station lies on 
	- Simply click the tile number to export it as a zip 
	- The primary export is a jp2 file, which requires photoshop (GIMP or adobe works) or a specialized photo editor

## OPEN STREET MAPS ANNOTATIONS (LINK TWO)

#### TO EXPORT (TO FIGMA):
	- Click share button (~ map should be at the 200ft (1:2350 scale) mark zoomed in for now) 
	- Exporting as SVG is broken (figma lags like crazy and the file itself is messed up currently as of September, 2026) so export as a PDF (which contains the actual svg within in))
	- *In Figma* Use the figma plugin **pdf.tomake.design** and import the SVG as a figma design (not a vector or image)
	- The output is a map frame where the vector graphics are kept in one group and the text is just outside of it (you'll see when importing it)
